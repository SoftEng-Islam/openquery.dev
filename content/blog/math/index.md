Math has a reputation for being scary. **This course is here to change that!** 🚀

Together we'll start with the very first numbers and climb, one small step at a time, all the way to university-level ideas like $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$. Every lesson is written like a story: a question, a bit of curiosity, and then the tools to answer it.

## How the Course Works

- 🧭 **Go in order.** Each lesson builds on the one before it.
- 🐢 **Go at your own pace.** There are no deadlines and no tests.
- ✏️ **Try it yourself.** Grab a pen and play with the examples.

## Start Here 👇

New to the course? Begin with [the Introduction](/math/intro), then move on to the first section.

## The Roadmap

1. [**Arithmetic**](/math/arithmetic): numbers, operations, fractions, and percentages
2. [**Algebra**](/math/algebra): finding the unknown with $x$
3. **Geometry**: shapes, angles, and area
4. **Trigonometry**: the secrets of $\sin\theta$ and $\cos\theta$
5. **Probability and Statistics**: making sense of chance
6. **Calculus**: change, motion, and infinity
7. **Linear Algebra**: vectors and matrices
8. **Advanced Topics**: number theory, proofs, and discrete math
